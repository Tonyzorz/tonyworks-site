Balance & leaderboard fixes

  • Very large leaderboard scores display correctly again instead of appearing as unreadable text.
  • World Gate Hard Mode was re-checked from end to end and rebalanced.
  • Critical-hit and dodge pressure now tightens as players move through Greece, the Military Zone, and Heaven.

The Maze opens a new Normal Mode chapter

  • The new route begins in the Maze and continues through Ice, America, and the Amazon.
  • The companion atlas already catalogs all 27 planned maps: 12 Maze maps and 5 maps in each onward region.
  • Upcoming maps, monsters, equipment, and bosses are clearly labeled so they do not look live before release.
2026-08-27 · Companion site update

Complete regional data, clearer guides, and corrected artwork

  • Refreshed the companion catalog to 595 route monsters, 999 public collection items, 31 bosses, 68 maps, 624 zones, 25 characters, and 62 achievements.
  • Added the complete Maze, Ice, America, and Amazon equipment and monster artwork, plus all six regional boss images.
  • Corrected distinct monster archetypes that previously shared artwork in the Rotunda and three Maze gate maps.
  • Reworked the player guides around first-run help, stuck-run recovery, unlock order, current live content, and the next chapter.
2026-08-20 · Current project update

World Gate hard mode rebalanced

  • Critical-damage resistance in World Gate hard mode now scales with your agility. Previously a single critical hit could end even the final boss fight regardless of your equipment.
  • The Archangel Sovereign now has more health and hits less hard, so the fight lasts long enough for your gear to matter.
  • Fixed dodge resistance in the first half of World Gate hard mode, where it previously had no effect.
  • Hard mode now takes roughly twice as many battles per map, and every region boss remains beatable. World Gate normal mode is unchanged.
2026-08-13 · Current project update

iPad readiness, World Gate balance, and calmer ads

  • Prepared the iOS project for a universal iPhone and iPad release, including iPad orientations, safe-area layout checks, and the complete App Store icon set.
  • Rebalanced the two World Gate accessory expansion slots: slot 11 costs 1Q gold and slot 12 costs 1Qi gold, using the same abbreviated number format shown elsewhere in the game.
  • Added a three-minute cooldown to the automatic run-over video ad after any full-screen video ad starts. Ads remain enabled; only the automatic run-over ad is temporarily skipped during that window.
  • Added launch-event and future World Gate event deep-link routing for App Store in-app events.
  • Refreshed the companion data and World Gate artwork from the current Unity project, including the latest enemy placements and the 25-character roster.
2026-08-07 · Current project update

New worlds, deeper stats, and battle presentation

  • Implemented the World Gate expansion: 19 maps across Japan, Greek, Military, and Heaven, with new route monsters, 5 bosses, equipment, and 6 new achievements. The entrance remains sealed in the current hotfix build, so the site labels this content as an upcoming preview.
  • Extended large-number progression beyond the previous integer ceiling with new stat suffixes for World Gate scaling.
  • Added and refreshed monster artwork across the expanded bestiary, plus the complete set of available map backgrounds and newer item art.
  • Reduced Normal Grassland monster and boss HP, ATK, and resistance pressure to roughly two-thirds of their prior values; other regions and modes are unchanged.
  • Improved combat readability with solid HP bars, distinctive damage numbers, stronger attack/combo/critical effects, varied regional battle backgrounds, a scrollable battle log, and a clearer result screen.
  • Corrected directional movement presentation across the character roster and improved HUD spacing, localized labels, analytics precision, and ad-request safeguards.
2026-07-27 · Version 1.0

Infinite Loot-Loop launches on the App Store

  • Infinite Loot-Loop is now available as a free download for iPhone and iPad.
  • The launch build requires iOS 15.0 or later and includes cloud save, offline play, and complete Normal and Hard Mode progression systems for the open main route. The current project roster contains 25 heroes.
  • The official companion wiki now links directly to the live App Store listing and includes a first-hand developer design note about the AP economy and balance process.

2026-07-16

Mobile services, encounters & result polish

  • Fixed iPhone touch-and-hold movement: hold an empty part of the map to move, drag to steer, and release to stop.
  • Holding near the character now fills the Encounter Gauge, letting players seek a battle without leaving their current spot.
  • UI buttons and open menus now correctly block movement without decorative HUD layers swallowing map touches.
  • Gold purchases now require Purchase / Cancel confirmation, and unaffordable item, character, and slot buttons are disabled.
  • Improved rewarded-ad recovery for revives and run-over gold, banner positioning between runs, and App Store purchase availability feedback.
  • Redesigned the premium Store, item-drop result card, and equipment-upgrade indicator for clearer visual hierarchy.
  • Corrected normal item drops to one shared 3% roll per victory, and made recommended equipment stand out with a dedicated BEST badge.
  • Improved mobile readability for EXP and permanent stats, added clear Set purchase feedback, and reduced the overworld character size by 10%.
  • Rebalanced permanent item bonuses to 1% on the first copy and 3% at full completion, with existing saves recalculated from owned items.
  • Added full-edge safe-area padding for notches and rounded phone corners, completed battle prompt translations, and standardized Store purchase-button heights.
  • Added an optional translucent arrow-control pad for held movement, with a persistent on/off toggle in Settings.
  • Gold, EXP, and ATK zone bonuses now use distinct colors, with an ultra-rare rainbow Gold x7 bonus.
  • After the first boss victory, the game makes a one-time request for Apple’s native review prompt.
  • Improved Forest Upper Gate and Ashen Gate doorway detection, restored the Volcanic-to-Void route in both directions, and focused Witch’s Peak entry on its top-left cave.
  • Achievements now have a distinctive colored tab deck for All, Milestones, Combat, Collection, Normal, and Hard categories.
  • Erase Data now clearly covers both local and cloud gameplay saves, safely retries an offline cloud deletion on the next online launch, and keeps restorable Store entitlements.
  • Added edge-aware camera framing so entrances at the top and bottom of each map stay visible above the Set controls and lower HUD.
2026-07-05

Stability & localization pass

  • Fixed a hard freeze caused by corrupted enemy EXP values; level-up processing is now capped and EXP/gold are modeled per expected level.
  • Comprehensive 15-language localization sweep with a new re-localizing text component (boss / character / item / map names and the stat guide).
  • Main-menu Store button fix and a new character nameplate banner.
2026-06-12 · Session 42

Balance & polish

  • Adventure Point economy reconciled: 30 AP base · 4–6 AP on death · +3 AP per boss. Death now keeps your level, EXP, and gold.
  • Achievement stat bonuses are now derived from the unlocked set (no inflation, syncs across devices).
  • Full Reset now wipes all permanent progress.
  • Forest doorways re-laid (FR_03/FR_04) with zone levels flipped; central anti-stuck spawn corridor added.
  • Battle panel forced full-screen with a run-over tap guard; live derived stats in the stat panel; equipment icon blur fix.
  • Biome BGM name-matching fix; several doorway/trigger retunes across Forest, Volcano, Desert and Underwater.
