Version 7.0.0 · Wiki updated 8 October 2026

Mexico, Candy & SuperMarket

  • Three new Normal Mode regions, each reached from Cloud Plaza and each with three large maps and its own boss.
  • Museum Hard opens Modern 1, Modern 2, Future 1 and Future 2 from the Atrium. These are different wings with their own enemies, gear and bosses.
  • Stress and Burn have separate resistance effects. Bring Stress Resist for the Modern wings, Slow Heal for Future 1, and both for Future 2.
  • Character Mastery grows through route victories: regular wins grant 1 XP, elites 2, and bosses 10, with reduced credit far behind your progression. Each level adds 3% total final-stat growth, split according to that character's style.
  • Lucky Drop lets LUC award extra copies. Drop bonus areas add +2%, +3%, or a rare +7%.
  • Equipment collection bonuses now rise from 1% at the first copy to 20% at ten copies. Accessory bonuses remain 0%, 2%, and 5% at one, two, and three copies.
  • Gold Draw boss-item chance can reach 3%. Heavy DEF builds have been adjusted. Lifesteal uses your highest effect instead of stacking.
  • Death Mode gains a cheaper, stronger skill tree, 40% more skill points, Utility skills, persistent purchased slots, and item locks.
  • New portals return to the start of the Maze.
Version 6.0.0 · The Museum

The original Museum is available

Normal Mode includes the Atrium, Stone Age, Civilization I, Civilization II and the Modern Area of Study. The 25-map Museum uses Burn and regeneration, countered by Burn Resist and Slow Heal. Stone, Egypt and The Temple also have Hard Mode routes. The catalog includes the subsequent Museum balance adjustments.

2026-09-26 · Version 5.5.2

Stat points and subscriptions, fixed

  • Fixed: stat allocation. Since v5.5.0 the Confirm button on the stat screen did nothing, so points you placed were never committed. It works again on both platforms.
  • Fixed (Android): subscriptions switching themselves off. A Battle Speed x2 or Remove Ads subscription could be cancelled in the game by one failed check with Google Play — often the one that runs the moment the purchase sheet closes. A paid subscription now stays active through a failed check and ends only once the store has stopped confirming it.
  • Settings › Data now shows each subscription’s status, so if a purchase ever does not apply, you can see where it stopped.
  • Android shipped 26 September; the iPhone and iPad update followed on 27 September.
2026-09-24 · Version 5.5.1

Void echoes, a fairer Gold Draw, and boss rewards corrected

  • Fixed: keys in the Void. The Void’s echoes of The Unmoved, The Bound King and The Empty Seat had lost their monster type, so the Stone, Egypt and Temple keys did nothing against them. They count again.
  • The Gold Draw prefers what you have never had. Gear you do not own yet is drawn about ten times as often as gear you already hold. Duplicates still turn up, just far less often.
  • Boss rewards corrected. From the Graveyard onward, hard-mode bosses were paying gold and EXP far beyond what they were meant to — one kill could buy a later region’s shop. Every boss now pays in line with the zone it stands in; a handful of normal-mode bosses are trimmed too.
  • Egypt and The Temple re-priced. Their gear costs more — about twice as much in Egypt and about ten times as much in The Temple — to match the pace of the ring.
  • Fixed: secrets and their achievements. If you found a secret before its achievement existed, it now unlocks the next time you open the game.
  • The Cloud Plaza is shown by name in travel and leaderboard lists instead of as “CL01”.
  • Android: the game now also installs on older 32-bit (ARMv7) phones.
2026-09-22 · Version 5.5.0

Fast Travel & Better Loot

  • Fast Travel. Jump to any region you have already opened, straight from the menu. Unlocks with Death Mode; not available inside a Death run.
  • Drop rates up across the game. Field 3% → 5% (hard 2% → 3%); bosses 1% → 3% (hard 0.75% → 2%).
  • Duplicates hurt less. The penalty for copies you already own is halved, so a collection stops stalling near the end.
  • LUC matters more. It pays up to four times its old ceiling for drops, and keeps growing the deeper you go.
  • Elite monsters drop far more. Bosses you have beaten can reappear in stronger forms with far better drops.
  • The Void no longer crowds its pool with foes you have finished, and it keeps its own pity counter.
  • Paid bonuses raised with everything else: Loot Boost +0.75% → +1.5%, Lucky Hunter +0.5% → +1%.
  • The result screen closes on a tap anywhere except the Fight button. Loadouts can be renamed, and the name shows on the top bar. Your Power score now matches what the leaderboard measures.
  • Fixed: locked doors named the wrong boss. Every gate now names the boss it actually needs.
2026-09-16 · Version 5.0.0

Epoch 4: The Cloud Plaza

  • A new route opens. A stair on the west side of the World Gate leads up to the Cloud Plaza, a second hub town with a shop and a save point. No encounters there.
  • The stone arch opens onto Stone; from there Stone, Egypt and The Temple form a ring you walk round — 24 maps, 132 monsters, and three bosses: The Unmoved, The Bound King and The Empty Seat.
  • The new road is not kind to the empty-handed. Something the hard route drops is worth carrying on.
  • The hard route grows. Hard mode now continues past the Graveyard — Korea, London and Monochrome each gain a hard half.
2026-09-10 · Version 4.0.3

The Void & the Maze (Hard)

  • Fully-looted monsters return to the Void rarely rather than disappearing from it, so the pool stops thinning as you complete it. Bosses show up there less often.
  • The Void is capped per item: 1 copy of a boss drop, 2 of an accessory, 5 of other gear. Everything past that has to come from the monster’s own map. The drop panel reads “VOID MAX” once you are there.
  • Some Void encounters are now hollow — “204 No Content”. They fight and pay gold and EXP like any other battle, but they carry no items.
  • Hard Ice, America and Amazon are reachable. Those three doors needed a key boss that was never the one named on the message, and behind it sat a requirement for Normal-mode clears that was never meant to be there.
  • Eight Maze items could not be obtained at all. They drop now.
2026-09-09 · Version 4.0.2

World Gate rebalance, and the win streak you could never see

  • Winning battles in a row multiplies gold and EXP up to 2×, and it was never shown anywhere. The multiplier now appears on the map, in battle and on the victory screen. A loss resets it to zero, which is why rewards dropped off after a death.
  • World Gate is easier in both modes: all five bosses eased, and monster resistances lowered across Japan, Greece and the Military Yard. Nothing was made harder.
  • “Equip Best” no longer unequips Chaos Heart, and gear AP applies the moment the accessory is worn instead of needing a re-equip.
  • Boss relic effects were stacking with no ceiling. Heartwood’s lifesteal is now 3% Normal / 5% Hard, Nine-Dragon adds one shield layer per copy, and First Dead’s dodge sits inside the 20% cap. Nothing already held was removed.
  • Shield is back to a full 50% of max HP.
  • The +20% gold reward was paying less than 20%: salvage gold was never counted toward the run total. Gold exchanged from diamonds now counts as well.
  • The Void map no longer sends monsters whose drops you have already completed, and a Void boss now offers both of its unique drops — the second one could never appear there before.
  • “CRITICAL!” and “COMBO!” appeared in English in every language. Ads return to the preferred network after a temporary outage instead of staying on the fallback.
2026-09-07 · Version 4.0.0

Everything to Monochrome

  • The route past the Amazon now runs nine legs and four new regions: the Graveyard (10 maps), Korea (8), London (8) and Monochrome (20), ending in three bosses at the end of everything.
  • Hard Mode mirrors for the Maze, Ice, America and the Amazon, each with its own monsters and gear.
  • Every stat earns a second job. ATK: combo chains grow a combo multiplier, so past the first tier every extra hit multiplies like a critical. HP: shields come in layers, each a full pool. DEF: blocks reflect harder tier by tier, and a DEF-focused build unlocks Guard tiers that cut every hit taken.
  • Going all-in on one stat is a real build — every all-in build is measured to land within 25% of a balanced one.
  • Gold Draw: every coin staked raises the chance toward 100%, and overflow pays more — stake 100× the ask for a second item, 10,000× for a third.
  • A region’s shop and item list appear after your first kill there. The last map of each region opens a portal back once its boss falls.
  • Difficulty is the only gate. Every door opens when you can survive what is behind it.
