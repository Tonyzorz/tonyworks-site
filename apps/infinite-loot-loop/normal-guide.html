How a Normal run works

Starting AP
30 + bonuses
Regular victory
−1 AP
Boss victory
+3 AP
Defeat / retreat
−4 AP
  • A new run resets level, EXP, gold, and allocated stat points. Equipment, unlocked slots, collections, achievements, and permanent bonuses remain.
  • An ordinary defeat does not reset your current level, EXP, gold, or allocated stats. The run continues if AP remains.
  • A revive refunds that battle's defeat cost. Winning afterward still follows the normal victory rule.
  • Every level needs current level × 150 EXP and grants 4 stat points.
  • Enemy kill streak rewards rise by 10% per kill, up to a 2× multiplier at 10 kills.
Route principleA boss win gives AP instead of charging the regular 1 AP. Farm only when the EXP, gold, or possible drop is worth delaying the next boss.

Region-by-region plan

  1. Grassland: learn the encounter gauge and secure basic survivability. Its Normal enemies and boss use the reduced beginner balance, so this is the safest place to learn AP pacing.
  2. Forest: replace starter equipment, begin using saved loadouts, and buy slots 7 and 8 when their 10,000 and 50,000 gold costs fit your run.
  3. Volcanic: enemy resistances make one-dimensional builds less reliable. Keep enough HP and DEF to survive spikes while ATK, AGI, and LUC support faster clears.
  4. Desert: check enemy armor pierce and weaken values. A previous region's safe build may need newer gear before the longer branch and its six bosses.
  5. Underwater: complete the last six open Normal bosses. Treat Aqua Liege as the final readiness check before Hard Mode, not as an ordinary field encounter.

Current stat rules

The starting base is 100 HP, 10 ATK, 10 DEF, 5 AGI, and 5 LUC. One allocated point gives +5 HP or +1 to ATK, DEF, AGI, or LUC.

StatMain valueImportant current behavior
HPSurvive more damageReceives the level-power percentage bonus
ATKNormal, critical, and combo damageReceives the level-power percentage bonus
DEFFlat damage reductionReceives level power; enemies may ignore a share
AGICritical chance/damage, combo, dodgeDoes not receive the level-power percentage
LUCGold, combo, dodge, small drop nudgeDoes not receive the level-power percentage
Level powerFull HP, ATK, and DEF now gain 2.35 × ln(1 + level / 100)%. This includes base, equipment, permanent, achievement, and character stats. Item percentage bonuses also apply to the full stat total. AGI and LUC stay outside this multiplier.
  • Critical chance starts from AGI × 0.0015; critical damage starts at 1.5× and gains AGI × 0.00075, before enemy resistance.
  • Combo chance is driven by your allocated ATK points. Overflow becomes combo tiers, but combat allows at most 3 extra hits.
  • Dodge comes from AGI (about 0.035% per point) and is capped at 20% after resistance.
  • LUC adds 0.1% gold per point. Its direct drop nudge is much smaller: +0.0004 percentage points per LUC, capped at +0.6 percentage points.
AllocationThere is no universal ratio. Add ATK when fights are safe but slow, HP/DEF when bosses can end the fight, and AGI/LUC when their offensive and economy effects remain effective after the current enemy's resistance.

Loot, shop, and permanent growth

  • A regular Normal kill usually has one 3% shared drop budget, distributed among all eligible items. It is not a separate 3% roll for every item.
  • Duplicate decay makes additional copies progressively less likely. Capped items leave the pool and their weight is redistributed.
  • Boss item chance begins around 1% before character, equipment, hunt, and LUC bonuses.
  • The Normal shop is global and uses curated live-route equipment. You cannot buy a shop item you already own; additional copies come from drops.
  • You start with 6 equipment slots. Normal Mode can unlock slot 7 for 10,000 gold and slot 8 for 50,000 gold.
  • There are five saved equipment loadouts. Set 1 is free; Sets 2–5 cost 8,000, 250,000, 8,000,000, and 200,000,000 gold. They are loadouts, not matching-set combat bonuses.

The 20-boss Hard Mode checklist

Hard Mode unlocks only after all 20 open main-route bosses have been defeated in Normal Mode.

  • Grassland: Defeat Ancient Golem.
  • Forest: Defeat Bone Dragon Jr, Dark Treant, Soul Reaper, and Frost Witch.
  • Volcanic: Defeat Inferno Drake, Lava Titan, and Chaos Beast.
  • Desert: Defeat Crystal Elemental, Cursed Armor, Sun Beast, Tidal Guardian, Eternal King, and Desert Pharaoh.
  • Underwater: Defeat Deep Sea Horror, Thunder Oni, Golden Mimic, Void Stalker, Giant Clam, and Aqua Liege.

What unlocks after the main route

The game now runs from the Grassland to Monochrome. Finishing one phase prepares or unlocks the next, and difficulty is the only gate — every door opens once you can survive what is behind it.

  1. Normal main route: defeat the 20 bosses from Grassland through Underwater to unlock Hard Mode.
  2. Hard main route: defeat those 20 bosses again in Hard Mode to open the World Gate.
  3. Normal World Gate: clear Japan, Greece, the Military Zone, and Heaven, ending with all five World Gate bosses.
  4. Hard World Gate: the same nineteen maps return at a higher tier once all five Normal World Gate bosses are defeated.
  5. The Maze chapter: the Maze opens from the World Gate hub and branches into Ice, America and the Amazon, in Normal and then in Hard.
  6. The endgame: the hub's east gate opens onto the Graveyard, and from there the route runs through Korea and London to Monochrome, interleaved with the Hard mirrors of the Maze chapter. See Past the World Gate.
Where should I go?If Hard Mode is still locked, finish the 20 Normal main-route bosses. If the World Gate is locked, finish those 20 bosses in Hard. If Hard World Gate is locked, clear its five bosses in Normal.

Every stat has a second job

These secondaries scale with the points you allocate, not with your gear, and monster resistance reduces them. Concentrating points in one stat earns extra tiers for that stat; spreading them evenly earns none.

  • HP – Shield. Every battle starts with a shield worth up to 50% of your max HP, absorbed before your health. More HP points add extra shield layers — blue, dark blue, orange, violet, red — each a full pool that breaks before the next takes over. Past the World Gate there is no limit on layers.
  • ATK – Combo. A chance at up to 3 extra hits. Past the first combo tier every extra hit multiplies like a critical, up to 4×.
  • DEF – Block, Reflect and Guard. A chance to halve a hit (max 20%), and a blocked hit throws part of the damage back, harder with each reflect tier (up to 4). Putting most of your points into DEF unlocks Guard tiers past the World Gate: each cuts every hit you take by 10%, compounding, with no limit.
  • AGI – Crit and Dodge. Crit damage starts at 1.5× and caps at 4×. Dodge is about 0.035% per point and caps at 20%. Crit chance above 100% becomes crit tiers, each multiplying again, up to 5.
  • LUC – Gold and drops. Gold rises 0.1% per point. Drop rate climbs toward +0.5% and then very slowly to +0.75%.

Going all-in on one stat is a real build: every all-in build is measured to land within 25% of a balanced one.

Before entering Hard Mode

  • Upgrade a complete Normal endgame build rather than relying on a single high-stat item.
  • Unlock the two Normal equipment expansions and prepare separate damage, survival, and drop loadouts.
  • Keep useful boss drops and permanent collection bonuses; they remain available when a new run resets temporary growth.
  • The World Gate is live after its progression requirements are met, opening Japan, Greek, Military, and Heaven. The Maze continues from that hub into Ice, America and the Amazon, and the hub's east gate opens onto the Graveyard, Korea, London and Monochrome.
