Updated 4 August 2026 · Stock and prices come from the current game-data export

Choose the mode before comparing prices

Normal and Hard Mode use different equipment pools and very different price scales. Use the mode switch first so every result belongs to the run you are planning. The page remembers your selected mode across the wiki, making it easier to move between the Shop, Items, Monsters, Bosses, and Maps without repeatedly changing the setting.

Only equipment currently marked as purchasable appears here. Drop-only rewards, boss-exclusive gear, unreleased stock, and items disabled for shops remain in the full Items library instead of being mixed into the merchant list.

Filter by the slot that needs help

Use Equipment to narrow the catalog to Weapon, Armor, Helmet, Shoes, or Accessory. Price sorting helps identify affordable upgrades, while main-stat sorting highlights the largest immediate gains. Area filtering shows what can be purchased along a particular route.

Select any card to open the complete equipment record, including effects, permanent collection bonuses, set membership, and acquisition sources. A high price is not automatically the best purchase: compare the main stat and effects against the problem your current build needs to solve.
