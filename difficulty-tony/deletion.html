Data Deletion - Difficulty: Tony

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This page explains how to delete the data of Difficulty: Tony, made by Tony Works (tonyzorz@naver.com). The Game has no accounts of its own. The only data on our servers is your leaderboard data, made when you sign in with Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam.

Delete in the Game (fastest)

  1. Open Difficulty: Tony.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete data, then Delete.

This deletes all your leaderboard data from our servers at once. You need an internet connection for it. If you are offline, the Game tells you, and you can try again later.

What is deleted

  • Your entries on every leaderboard (times, falls, map times, game version).
  • Your display name and the one-way hash of your platform player ID.
  • Your review flags and your sessions.
  • Your player record. If you sign in again later, you start as a new, empty player.

Your climb on your device is not deleted by this. It stays, so you can keep climbing.

Delete the data on your device

Uninstall the Game. This removes your saved climb, settings, achievements and the local copy of your unlock. A purchase stays with your App Store or Google Play account; use Restore after a reinstall to get it back.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the Game, email tonyzorz@naver.com with:

  • Subject line: Data Deletion - Difficulty: Tony.
  • Your leaderboard name and your platform (iPhone/iPad, Android or Steam).

We delete your leaderboard data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What we do not hold

We hold no email address, payment details, advertising ID or device ID for the Game. Purchase records are kept by the Apple App Store, Google Play or Steam and are managed there. Your Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam account is managed by that platform.
