Data Deletion - Tony: Day One

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This page explains how to delete the data of Tony: Day One, made by Tony Works (tonyzorz@naver.com). The Game has no accounts of its own. The only data on our servers is your leaderboard data, made when you sign in with Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam.

Delete in the Game (fastest)

  1. Open Tony: Day One.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete data, then Delete.

This deletes all your leaderboard data for this Game from our servers at once. You need an internet connection for it. If you are offline, the Game tells you, and you can try again later.

What is deleted

  • Your entries on every leaderboard (times, falls, chapter times, game version).
  • Your display name and the one-way hash of your platform player ID.
  • Your review flags and your sessions.
  • Your player record. If you sign in again later, you start as a new, empty player.

Your climb on your device is not deleted by this. It stays, so you can keep climbing.

Delete the data on your device

Uninstall the Game. This removes your saved climb, settings, achievements, the comment cards you found and the local copy of your unlock. A purchase stays with your App Store or Google Play account; use Restore after a reinstall to get it back.

On iPhone and iPad, an optional copy of your saved climb is also kept in your own iCloud, and it survives an uninstall (so a reinstall can continue). We cannot see or delete it. To remove it, turn off iCloud for the Game, or delete the Game's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

On Android, an optional copy of your saved climb is kept as a Google Play Games saved game in your own Google account, and it survives an uninstall too. We cannot see or delete it. To remove it, open the Google Play Games app: Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data → Delete individual game data, and delete Tony: Day One.

On Steam, a copy of your saved climb and achievements file is kept in your own Steam Cloud, and it survives an uninstall. We cannot see or delete it. To remove it, turn off Steam Cloud for the Game in Steam (Properties → General → Steam Cloud) and delete the Game's local files, or remove them through Steam's own account pages.

Play statistics (iPhone and iPad)

The play statistics sent to Google Analytics for Firebase carry no name or player ID, so we cannot find yours to delete them by hand. Turn them off in Settings → Account → Analytics: nothing more is sent, and what was sent is deleted by Google Analytics after 2 months.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the Game, email tonyzorz@naver.com with:

  • Subject line: Data Deletion - Tony: Day One.
  • Your leaderboard name and your platform (iPhone/iPad, Android or Steam).

We delete your leaderboard data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What we do not hold

We hold no email address, payment details, advertising ID or device ID for the Game. Purchase records are kept by the Apple App Store, Google Play or Steam and are managed there. Your Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam account is managed by that platform.
