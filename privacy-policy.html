Privacy Policy - Infinite Loot Loop

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Infinite Loot Loop ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play. The Game is operated by Tony Works (tonyzorz@naver.com).

1. Offline-first gameplay

The Game is designed to work offline. You can play normal gameplay, earn items, and save progress locally on your device without an internet connection. Some optional features require internet access, including cloud save sync, advertisements, crash reporting, and purchase verification or restore.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create a separate game account and we do not collect your name, postal address, or precise location. The Game and the third-party services it uses may process:

  • Device and diagnostic data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, logs, and crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics. This is used to find bugs and improve stability.
  • Website analytics data: when you visit tonyworks.co.kr, Google Analytics may process information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source, and interaction events. We use this information to understand site usage and improve the companion wiki.
  • Advertising and mediation data: AppLovin MAX mediates advertising from AppLovin and participating demand partners, currently including Google AdMob and Unity Ads. Depending on your consent choices, device settings, applicable law, and network availability, these services may process IP address; approximate location derived from IP; device, app, browser, and network information; advertising or vendor identifiers when available; consent and privacy signals; ad requests, impressions, clicks, and reward completion; and fraud or security signals. Third-party measurement partners may also process limited data to measure ad delivery. The Game does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS, so it does not intentionally obtain IDFA for cross-app tracking.
  • Purchase data: in-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive purchase receipts or transaction information so purchases can be verified, restored, and protected from duplicate grants. We do not receive or store payment card details.
  • Cloud save data: if cloud save is used, gameplay progress is stored on Amazon Web Services and associated with an anonymous player identifier or Apple Game Center ID. Cloud save contains gameplay data such as level, items, achievements, map state, and progression.
  • Paid entitlement data: purchased diamonds, Remove Ads, purchased characters, and processed purchase transaction IDs are stored separately from cloud save so paid items can be restored safely.
  • Support correspondence: if you contact support, we receive the email address, message content, and device/app details you choose to send.

3. How we use and share information

  • Run the Game and save your progress locally or through optional cloud save.
  • Verify, grant, and restore in-app purchases.
  • Prevent duplicate purchase grants or purchase abuse.
  • Request, select, display, cap, and measure advertisements and rewarded-ad completion.
  • Share the advertising data and privacy signals described above with AppLovin and eligible mediated demand, measurement, and fraud-prevention partners so they can deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Measure companion-wiki usage and improve website navigation and content.
  • Respond to support requests.

5. Your choices

  • Offline play: you may play without using cloud save. Local saves remain on your device unless you delete the app data.
  • Cloud save: cloud save is used only when online sync is available. You may erase cloud gameplay data in the Game or request deletion by contacting support.
  • Advertising consent: where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before MAX is initialized. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS or Android device settings. The Game does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Provider privacy requests: AppLovin, Google, and Unity provide their own privacy controls and request methods in the policies linked above. Those controls apply to data held independently by those providers.
  • Remove Ads: the Game offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase.
  • Data deletion: to request deletion of cloud save or entitlement data, contact tonyzorz@naver.com.

6. Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the Game: open Settings → Data → Erase Data to permanently delete your local gameplay save and your cloud gameplay save. If the device is offline, cloud deletion is queued and completes on the next online app launch. Store purchase and entitlement records are retained so eligible purchases can be restored.
  • Local only: deleting or uninstalling the app removes all local save data from your device.
  • By request: email tonyzorz@naver.com with the player ID shown in Settings → Data, and we will delete your cloud save and entitlement records, typically within 30 days.

Step-by-step instructions are also on our Data Deletion Request page. A request to Tony Works deletes data controlled by Tony Works; it does not automatically delete information independently controlled by AppLovin, Google, Unity, Apple, or an app store. Use the privacy controls offered by those providers and your device for that information.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Data retention

Local save data remains on your device until you delete it. Cloud save and entitlement records are kept while needed to provide save sync, purchase restore, support, and fraud prevention, and may be deleted on request where legally permitted. Diagnostic, analytics, and advertising data are retained according to the applicable third-party providers' own retention policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
